We offer a wide Range of Services

P.S Metallurgical & NDT Services (PSMN) delivers Non-Destructive Testing, Destructive Testing, Metallography & Analysis, and Certification & Training from our NABL-accredited lab in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, serving industrial clients across Telangana.

01

Non-Destructive Testing

PSMN provides Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) services in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, helping industrial clients detect flaws and verify material integrity without damaging components. Our ASNT Level II & III certified engineers carry out:

  • Ultrasonic Flaw Detection
  • Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging
  • Magnetic Particle Testing
  • Dye Penetrant Testing
  • Radiography Testing
02

Destructive Testing

Our destructive testing lab in Hyderabad evaluates the mechanical strength, durability, and composition of metals and welds by testing samples to failure, giving precise data for quality assurance and compliance:

  • Mechanical Testing (Yield, Tensile, Elongation)
  • Hardness Test
  • Izod & Charpy Impact Tests
  • Failure Analysis
  • Chemical Testing (SPECTRO MAX)
03

Metallography & Analysis

Our metallography lab in Kukatpally, Hyderabad examines the internal structure of metals under magnification to assess material quality, composition, and manufacturing defects, including:

  • Microstructure & Macrostructure Testing
  • Inclusion Rating, Grain Size
04

Certification & Training

PSMN also supports quality professionals in Hyderabad with certification, training, and third-party inspection, backed by our ASNT Level II & III credentials since 2006:

  • WPS & PQR Certification
  • Third Party Inspection
  • ASNT Level II Training Classes

Frequently Asked Questions

Ultrasonic flaw detection uses high-frequency sound waves to find internal cracks, voids, and defects in metal components without damaging them. PSMN performs ultrasonic flaw detection and ultrasonic thickness gauging from our NABL-accredited lab in Kukatpally, Hyderabad.

Magnetic Particle Testing (MPT) detects surface and near-surface cracks in ferromagnetic materials, while Dye Penetrant Testing (PT) reveals surface-breaking defects using a visible or fluorescent dye. Both are part of PSMN's Non-Destructive Testing services.
